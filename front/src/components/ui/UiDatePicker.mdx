# Date picker

Chooses one day. Arrows step a day back and forth around a field naming the chosen day ("poniedziałek, 09.11.2026");
the field opens a month calendar. Bind the day with `v-model`, as a `Temporal.PlainDate` (ADR 0033); `min` and `max` limit
the days that can be chosen, the arrows are disabled at the ends and the calendar disables the days beyond them. A
"dziś" button goes back to today, set apart from the arrows by a line so it isn't tapped by mistake.

Nothing moves while clicking through days: the field is as wide as the longest day name of the week, and "dziś" stays
in place, disabled on today. `:today-button="false"` leaves it out.

**Today** is the app's: the day in the provider's time zone, provided by the application (`provideToday`) and read
with `useToday()`. Without a provided one it is today in the device's time zone. The `today` prop overrides it, as
the stories do.

It belongs to the family of `UiToggleGroup`: the same `ui-control` look, `variant` (`normal` black, `primary` blue)
and `size` (`small`, `normal`, `large`; only `large` is 44px high, the comfortable size to tap).

## Keyboard

The calendar follows the date picker dialog pattern of the WAI-ARIA practices: the arrows move a day or a week, Home
and End go to the week's ends, Page Up and Page Down move a month (a year with Shift), Enter or Space picks the day
and Escape closes the calendar. Focus returns to the field when it closes.

## Calendar

The month calendar is `UiCalendar`, which can be used on its own, for example as a filter. It always shows six weeks
from Monday, so it keeps its height. On its own it has an outline like the other controls; `flat` leaves it out
where something frames the calendar already (the picker's popup, a filter panel). Its days come from `buildMonth` in `src/time/`, one cell model per day: what a day shows (later, for
example, whether there is service on it) belongs in that model.
