# Section settings

A gear button for the `#actions` of a `UiSection`. It opens its slot in a dialog anchored to itself, named by its
`label`, and closes it on a click outside or Escape. The keyboard goes into the dialog when it opens, and back to the
gear on Escape. `size="large"` makes the gear a 44px tap target on a phone, for a gear outside a section's header.
