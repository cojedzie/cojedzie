# Timetable

A stop's timetable of one day, built from small parts. The components only lay out what the builders of
`src/timetable` compute from the day's departures (`fetchTimetableDay`): grouping, footnote letters and hours
are theirs.

## The page

`StopTimetable` takes the day's departures and the provider's time zone; the day, the view and the grouping are
`v-model`s, kept by the page. For a stop's timetable of one line (`lineFixed`) there is no grouping to choose.
